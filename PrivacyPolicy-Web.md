1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Clutch City AppWorks LLC ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website at clutchcityappworks.com and any related pages that link to this policy (the "Site").

This policy does not cover data collected inside our mobile apps (such as LottoLedger), which are subject to their own in-app privacy notices.


2. Information We Collect via Analytics

When you visit our Site, we use third-party analytics tools to understand how visitors use our pages and to monitor technical performance. These tools may collect:

- IP address and general location (e.g., city or region).
- Device and browser information (e.g., device type, operating system, browser type, screen resolution).
- Usage information (e.g., pages visited, links clicked, time spent on pages, error codes).
- Basic interaction data (e.g., how quickly pages load and render, whether certain features are used).

Some of this information (such as IP address and certain identifiers) may be considered "personal data" under applicable laws.

Google Analytics
We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our Site and to improve layout and content. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect usage information in a pseudonymized form.

Cloudflare Web Analytics & RUM (Real User Monitoring)
We use Cloudflare's analytics and RUM to monitor the technical performance and security of our Site, including page load speeds, error events, and network performance.

We do not combine this analytics information with other data to attempt to identify you as an individual.


3. How We Use This Data

We use the information described above for the following purposes:

- Performance and reliability: To monitor how quickly the Site loads, identify errors, and ensure it functions correctly for visitors.
- Feature planning and improvement: To understand which Site pages, tools, or resources are most used so we can improve them.
- Security and abuse prevention: To help detect and prevent bot traffic, fraud, and other malicious activity.

We do not use website analytics data to show you ads, build cross-site marketing profiles, or otherwise engage in "targeted advertising" on the Site.

We do not sell personal data collected on the Site.


4. How We Share Information

We may share information collected on the Site in the following limited ways:

- Service providers: With third-party vendors that provide analytics, performance, hosting, or security services for the Site (currently Google Analytics and Cloudflare). These vendors process data on our behalf and under our instructions.
- Legal and compliance: With law enforcement, regulators, or other parties when we believe disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect our rights, safety, or property, or that of others.
- Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or other corporate transaction involving Clutch City AppWorks LLC, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share personal data from the Site with third parties for targeted advertising.


5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

How We Use Cookies and Beacons

Clutch City AppWorks LLC does not use cookies on the Site for marketing, advertising, or user profiling. However:

- Google Analytics cookies may be used to distinguish unique visitors and sessions and to understand how visitors navigate the Site. These cookies typically expire automatically after a set period (for example, between 2 and 14 months, depending on Google's configuration and your browser).
- Cloudflare performance beacons (RUM) may be used to measure the performance and "health" of our Site during your visit. These technologies are used to monitor technical performance and do not track you across other websites.

Managing Your Preferences

- Browser settings: Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Doing so may impact some analytics functionality but should not prevent basic use of the Site.
- Google Analytics opt-out: You can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on (https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) to prevent your information from being used by Google Analytics.

Because we do not sell personal data from the Site or use it for targeted advertising, some state-law opt-out mechanisms (such as "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" links) are not applicable to this Site at this time.

If we ever begin using cookies or similar technologies for advertising or other non-analytics purposes, we will update this policy and, where required, provide appropriate consent and opt-out options.

The following rights may apply to you where required by law:

- Right to deletion: You can request that we delete personal data we have collected about you, subject to certain exceptions (for example, where we must retain data for legal obligations, fraud prevention, or security reasons).

  - We do not use personal data on the Site for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects for you.
